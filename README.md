# My Favorite Movie

## Iron Man 2

My favorite movie of all time is **Iron Man 2**.
Its *character development* is very compelling. The action sequences are innovative, the special effects still hold up, 
and the performances are incredible - especially Robert Downey Jr.'s portrayal of Tony Stark.

### Why I Like It
- Compelling character arc
- Innovative action sequences
- Great special effects
- Robert Downey Jr.'s iconic performance
- A perfect blend of humor and heart
